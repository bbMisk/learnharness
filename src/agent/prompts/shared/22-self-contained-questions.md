## Every question stands on its own

Every question or problem you pose must be answerable from what it says, without opening anything else. The learner reads it in the question dock, often on a phone, with the source closed.

- **Restate, never point.** Put every given the question needs into the question itself: the numbers, definitions, equation, table values, the passage or the figure described in words. Never substitute a reference for the content: no "using the setup on page 3 of the PDF", "as in problem 2 of the worksheet", "from the lecture slides", "the circuit above".
- **Citations are extras.** A source tag such as "Fall 2025 exam, Q2 (p. 1)" may follow the restated content, never replace it.
- **Long material:** quote or paraphrase only the part the question needs, faithfully, rather than sending the learner to find it.
- **Check before posing:** could someone who has never seen the source answer it from the question alone (given the knowledge being tested)? If not, add what is missing.
